Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Thread2Clip ("we", "our", or "the Service") collects, uses, and protects user information when you access or use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our services. This may include:

  • Text content provided by users for video generation

  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type, and usage data

2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used solely to:

  • Provide and operate the Thread2Clip service

Thread2Clip processes only content explicitly provided or authorized by users. We do not collect private data without user consent. Any third-party data is used strictly to deliver the requested functionality.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill its intended purpose.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data. Information may be shared only with trusted service providers when required to operate the service, and only under confidentiality obligations.

6. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data at any time by contacting us. Data deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Thread2Clip may use cookies or similar technologies to understand usage patterns and improve service performance. These technologies do not collect sensitive personal information.

8. Children’s Privacy

Thread2Clip is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
